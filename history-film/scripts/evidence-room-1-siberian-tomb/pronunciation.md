# Pronunciation guide (Part 1)

## Tyva
- Say: **TEE-vah** (rhymes with "Eva" without the E).
- Basis: Merriam-Webster gives "Tyva" as "ˈti-və" (TEE-vuh) and lists Tuva as the variant. https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/tuva
- Other forms you may hear: "TOO-vuh" for the spelling Tuva (English Wikipedia, /ˈtuːvə/) and a Russian stress on the second syllable (Tyvá). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuva
- Recommended: use "TEE-vah" every time, and show "Tyva (also spelled Tuva)" on screen once so viewers who know it as "Tuva" are not confused.
- It is a region and republic in southern Siberia (Tuva / Tyva Republic).

## Tunnug
- NO authoritative pronunciation source found (two searches). Treat any guess as UNVERIFIED.
- Best-guess approximation: **TOON-noog** ("oo" as in "moon", the first syllable stressed, short second "oog" as in "book"). The name is Tuvan, so English approximations will not be exact.
- To check: ask a Tuvan speaker, or listen to the study authors discussing the site in an interview or lecture (the dig is by Russian and Swiss/German archaeologists, e.g. Sadykov, Blochin, Caspari).
- Also known as "Arzhan 0" in the literature. Say "Tunnug 1" and then "Arzhan 0" if you want a second name that has a clearer pronunciation: **ar-ZHAHN** (zh as in "measure"). This one is also from general knowledge, not yet source-checked.
- Until it is checked: say the name the same way each time and add "(TOON-noog)" only if you are happy with it. We can also cut down how often you need to say it.

## Other names in the script
- **Scythian**: SITH-ee-un (first syllable like "Sith" in Star Wars is a common English habit, or SIH-thee-un; both appear in English dictionaries). Check: Merriam-Webster.
- **Herodotus**: huh-ROD-uh-tus.
- **Radiocarbon**, **THC**, **CBN** (say the letters: C-B-N).
- **BCE**: say "B-C-E" or "before the common era".
