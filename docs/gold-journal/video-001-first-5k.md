## 1. Voice: the Keeper

The narrator is **the Keeper**, the voice of the Gold Journal itself. Separate from James's Notes. No cross-over.

**Personality:** dry, unhurried, quietly confident, a field-journal mentor. The opposite of the shouting money guru. He is amused by hype and never impressed by it.

**Devices that make it memorable (use consistently, every video):**
1. **Entry numbers.** Each chapter opens "Entry one", "Entry two" and so on, spoken, with a pen-scratch sound.
2. **Margin notes.** One short aside per chapter, delivered quieter and slightly lower, as if written in the margin. Marked *(margin)* below.
3. **The ledger line.** A closing sentence per chapter that is a plain rule, said slowly. Marked **Rule:**.
4. **No hype words:** never "secret", "hack", "easy", "guaranteed", "life-changing".

**ElevenLabs voice direction** (for `creative_design_voice`, or pick from `creative_list_voices`): *Mature male or female voice, mid-40s, warm low baritone/alto, measured pace, dry wit, slight smile in the tone, crisp consonants, documentary-narrator calm, never salesy.* Pace about 150 wpm, pauses of about 0.4 s before each "Rule:". Margin notes about 8% quieter and 5% slower. One take per block, as the repo already does for the James series.

## 2. Script

Format per beat: **VO**, then **Visual** (A = animation, R## = screen recording), then **On-screen text**.

### Cold open (0:00 to 0:45)

**VO:** "Entry one. Most videos about your first five thousand dollars online begin with a rented sports car. This one begins with arithmetic. Five thousand dollars is not a miracle. It is five sales of a thousand. Or fifty of a hundred. It is a number you can work backwards from, and that is what we are going to do. Five chapters. Five frameworks. A quick note before we start: I am a journal. This is education, not financial advice, nothing here is guaranteed, and most people who try this earn nothing at first. Some links in the description are affiliate links, which means I may earn a commission if you buy through them, at no cost to you. Now. Open a blank page."

- **Visual:** A, the Builder (Wren) stares at an empty ledger on a desk; the Keeper's cover glows open. Cut to **R01** (own empty income tracker showing $0) as Wren's POV.
- **On-screen text:** "FIRST $5K · THE HONEST ROADMAP" then "Education only. Not financial advice."

### Chapter 1: The 5K Equation (0:45 to 2:15)

**VO:** "Entry two. The five thousand dollar equation. Price times buyers equals five thousand. That is the whole thing. Pick a price, and the number of people you need appears. Fifty dollars a product needs one hundred buyers. Five hundred dollars needs ten. A thousand dollars needs five. Same destination, three very different roads. The first road is cheap and needs an audience. The second needs trust and a clear result. The third needs a skill and a few good conversations. Most people never do this sum. They chase 'make money online' as if it were a place instead of a number. Here is the exercise. Write your three versions on a page. Then ask one honest question: which of these could I actually reach, in my life, with the time I have? That is your starting road.

*(margin)* "A dream is a destination without arithmetic."

**Rule:** "Choose the equation before the business."

- **Visual:** A, coins and equation cards slide into Wren's ledger. **R02** (typing three formulas that each equal 5,000), **R03** (a product page at a modest price: own Gumroad draft), **R04** (a checkout in test mode).
- **On-screen text:** "PRICE × BUYERS = $5,000" with three cards: "$50 × 100", "$500 × 10", "$1,000 × 5". Label: "Hypothetical".

### Chapter 2: Why most people fail (2:15 to 4:15)

**VO:** "Entry three. Why most people stop. Not because they are lazy. Usually it is one of four leaks. Leak one: shiny objects. This week it is dropshipping, next week it is a newsletter, then it is a channel. Each switch resets the clock to zero. Leak two: no distribution. They build something, publish it quietly, and wait. Nobody can buy what nobody has seen. Leak three: wrong fit. A shy person forcing cold calls, or a perfectionist who needs to ship ten rough videos and ships none. Leak four is the sneaky one: quitting in the flat zone. Early work looks like nothing. Zero views, zero sales, for weeks. That is not proof it fails. It is what the start of compounding looks like. People leave one week before the line bends, and never find out where it would have gone.

*(margin)* "I have read many journals. Most of the abandoned ones stop at page nine."

**Rule:** "Failing slowly is still failing. Switching quickly is how it happens."

- **Visual:** A, the Drifter (Flick) hops from shiny object to shiny object while Wren keeps drawing one line. **R05** (30 open tabs, cursor hopping), **R06** (own new analytics page with zero views), **R07** (a habit tracker with gaps).
- **On-screen text:** "LEAK 1 SHINY OBJECTS · LEAK 2 NO DISTRIBUTION · LEAK 3 WRONG FIT · LEAK 4 QUITTING IN THE FLAT ZONE"

### Chapter 3: Choose the one that fits you (4:15 to 6:45)

**VO:** "Entry four. Choosing a business that suits you. Not the loudest one. Yours. Score each path from one to five on four things: skill, time, cash, and temperament. Skill is what you can already do. Time is the hours you truly have. Cash is what you can risk without flinching. Temperament is what you can keep doing when it is boring. Now the paths. Freelance work sells a skill you already have. It is the fastest road to a first dollar, and it needs nothing but a profile and some proof. Digital products, such as templates or short guides, take longer to build but can sell while you sleep, once people trust you. Affiliate content means recommending tools you actually use and earning a commission when someone buys through your link. It is slower, because trust takes time, and you must always disclose the link. But it is honest, it scales, and you do not need your own product. Local services are for people who like talking to people. Print on demand and ecommerce need more cash and more testing. None of these is the best. The best one is the one your score says you will still be doing in ninety days.

*(margin)* "Fashionable is not a fit."

**Rule:** "Match the road to the traveller."

- **Visual:** A, Wren tries on five hats (freelancer, maker, recommender, local, seller) while the Keeper's pages tally scores. **R08** (Google Trends comparing niches), **R09** (a freelance marketplace search, public page), **R10** (an affiliate programme's terms page, with no earnings shown), **R11** (building a simple template cover in a design tool), **R12** (setting up a draft product listing).
- **On-screen text:** Scorecard: "SKILL · TIME · CASH · TEMPERAMENT (1 to 5)". Five path cards. Small print: "Affiliate links are disclosed."

### Chapter 4: Raise your odds: one, one, ninety (6:45 to 8:15)

**VO:** "Entry five. How to raise your odds. One business. One channel. Ninety days. One business, so your effort stacks instead of scattering. One channel, such as a video channel or a single marketplace, so you learn one audience properly. Ninety days, because that is long enough to leave the flat zone and short enough to survive it. During that time you do not pivot because you are bored. You may change only on data, a small and specific thing that the numbers say. Put the ninety days on a calendar, in ink. Block the hours, the way you would for a doctor.

*(margin)* "A plan you can see gets done. A plan you can feel gets postponed."

**Rule:** "Narrow the road. Lengthen the time."

- **Visual:** A, Flick's signposts fall away and Wren walks one lit path with a ninety-day trail. **R13** (blocking a recurring 90-day slot in a calendar), **R14** (a content board with one channel and twelve planned items), **R15** (typing a niche into a search box to see autosuggest).
- **On-screen text:** "1 BUSINESS · 1 CHANNEL · 90 DAYS"

### Chapter 5: Consistency and always improving (8:15 to 9:45)

**VO:** "Entry six. Consistency is not intensity. It is the smallest action you can repeat when you do not feel like it. One post. Five messages. Thirty minutes of building. Pick a minimum and keep it, even on bad days. Then add the loop that makes it improve: ship, measure, fix. Ship something. Look at one number. Change one thing. Every Friday, fifteen minutes, three lines: what moved, what did not, and the one change for next week. Small changes, repeated, beat a brilliant plan that is abandoned. And a gentle warning. Your first month will probably look unimpressive. That is allowed. You are not behind, you are early.

*(margin)* "Compound interest is mostly patience wearing a suit."

**Rule:** "Ship. Measure. Fix. Repeat on Friday."

- **Visual:** A, a time-lapse of Wren's weeks, pages turning, the line slowly bending. **R16** (ticking a three-item daily checklist), **R17** (a Friday review doc filled in), **R18** (an own analytics chart, real data only or clearly labelled "sample"), **R19** (queuing a post in a scheduler; do not publish).
- **On-screen text:** "SHIP → MEASURE → FIX" and "Friday review: what moved · what didn't · one change".

### Outro (9:45 to 10:30)

**VO:** "Entry seven. Close the page. Choose your equation. Score your four things. Pick one business, one channel, and ninety days. Then do the small thing today. I make no promises about results, because nobody honestly can. What I can promise is that people who write the plan down tend to go further than people who only wish. If this was useful, the next entry goes deeper on choosing your first business. Everything I mentioned is linked below, some of it with affiliate links, and none of it is advice. This has been the Gold Journal. See you on the next page."

- **Visual:** A, Wren closes the ledger, a gold seal stamps the cover. **R20** (a Vibloid project review screen with the Approve button, the "nothing publishes without a human" rule shown as a joke beat).
- **On-screen text:** "NEXT ENTRY: CHOOSING YOUR FIRST BUSINESS" and a final line "Education only. Not financial advice."

## 4. Character visual brief (for Opus)

**Style.** Flat 2D vector animation, hand-inked outlines, paper texture. Palette: cream paper `#f5ecd6`, ink navy `#0e1726`, gold leaf `#d4a937`, accent teal `#2a7f7f` for the Builder's world, warm red `#c2493d` sparingly for warnings. Canvas 1920×1080 at 30 fps. Gold appears only on important objects: the journal, coins, the seal, the lit path.

**Cast** (unique to the Gold Journal, not James):

1. **The Keeper.** The gold-bound journal itself. It is not a face, it is a cover with a ribbon bookmark, an embossed sun-and-quill seal, and a pen that writes the margin notes. Reacts by opening, turning pages, and glowing slightly. The pen scratch is its sound cue.
2. **Wren, the Builder.** Gender-neutral beginner with a round head, messy short hair, a teal hoodie, and a small notebook always under one arm. Expressive eyebrows. Always the protagonist and never shown with money other than hypothetical coin icons.
3. **Flick, the Drifter.** A small gold-and-orange hummingbird-like character who darts between shiny objects (rocket, crypto coin, "ecom" crate). Never evil, only distracted. Leaves a trail of dropped half-finished pages.

**Motion rules.** Page turns of 0.4 s, ease-out. Coins and equation cards slide in with a small overshoot. Flick moves fast and in straight hops, Wren moves slowly and steadily. Keep character motion calm and avoid hype zoom effects. Every hypothetical number appears inside a paper tag labelled **Hypothetical**.

**Scenes to build (one per chapter, 20 to 40 s each):**

| Scene | Content |
|---|---|
| S0 Open | Empty ledger, Keeper cover glows open, Wren's pencil hovers |
| S1 Equation | Three equation cards slide into the ledger, coins stack to 5K, the road forks into three |
| S2 Leaks | Flick hops between shiny objects while Wren draws one line; four labelled leaks drip through a bucket; a flat line at zero then bends |
| S3 Fit | Wren swaps five hats; scorecard fills for each; one path glows |
| S4 One-One-Ninety | Flick's signposts fall away; Wren walks a single lit path across a 90-day calendar |
| S5 Loop | A circular Ship, Measure, Fix loop turns through Friday pages; the line slowly rises |
| S6 Close | Ledger closes, gold seal stamps, next-entry teaser tag |

**Transitions.** Recordings appear inside a paper frame with a slight tilt and gold corner tabs, so the real footage always reads as a "pasted page" in the journal.
