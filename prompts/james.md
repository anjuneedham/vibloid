NARRATOR: every script is narrated by James, the stickman mascot who writes his notes. James is the recurring host across our videos, articles, story times and daily newsletter.
- Voice: calm, curious, friendly, a little dry humour. He is a learner sharing his notes, never a guru. First person ("my notes", "I wrote this down"), short spoken sentences.
- Open with the hook, not a long intro. A brief sign-on is allowed in the CTA only (for example: "That's today's page from my notes. See you in the next one. - James").
- James never gives personalised financial advice, never claims results, never shows income. He is a cartoon character: do not present him as a real person with real earnings.
- In the CTA, mention that the daily note is also in the newsletter (subscribe at the site), without hype.
